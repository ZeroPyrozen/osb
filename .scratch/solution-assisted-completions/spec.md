# Completions that used the solution

Status: parked (left out of phase 2 of the learn review on 2026-11-08)

## Problem

The rest of finding F9 in the 27 October 2026 curriculum review. An exercise completed after "Show
solution" earns the full XP and looks exactly like one the learner solved on their own, so exercise
completion can't show skill.

## Already decided (phase 1, PR #15)

- "Show solution" unlocks after three failed checks of the learner's own code, after one such check once
  every hint has been shown, or straight away on an exercise the learner has already completed.
- A completion that used the solution still earns full XP. Changing that is this item.

## Still open

- How a completion records that the solution was shown, in browser progress and on the server (a new column
  and a migration).
- How much XP such a completion earns.
- Whether solving the exercise again later without the solution upgrades it.
- How the progress page shows how often a learner solves exercises on their own.
- Whether achievements such as "Hands on" count completions that used the solution.
