# 04 · Write five questions for the unassessed outcomes

Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## What

Add one question to each of these modules' knowledge checks, tagged with the outcome nothing assesses
today (the review's wording):

| Module | Outcome | Bloom level |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| What is a storyboard? | Compare the three ways of making a storyboard (Design tab, hand scripting, generators) and pick one for a job. | Understand |
| Scale, rotate and colour | Prepare images that stay sharp when scaled and smooth when rotated, within the 17,000,000-pixel limit. | Understand |
| Shorthand | Judge when shorthand makes a script harder to read. | Evaluate |
| Timing to music | Plan a storyboard around the song's structure and energy: sections, kiai, build-ups and drops. | Create |
| Variables and triggers | Explain trigger restarts, group numbers and why triggered sprites should be faded out. | Understand |

Write each question at its outcome's level where a multiple-choice question can reach it: a scenario to
judge rather than a rule to recall, with wrong choices drawn from real misconceptions. Spread the answer
positions and give every question an explanation.

## Open point: decide when this step starts

"Timing to music" has a **create**-level planning outcome, and one multiple-choice question can only partly
assess it. Choose one:

- (a) Reword it to the level today's lessons teach, for example "Match effects to the song's sections,
  kiai, build-ups and drops" (analyse). The planning outcome then comes back with phase 3's planning lesson
  and phase 4's Intermediate project.
- (b) Keep it, and accept that the question assesses it only partly, like the review's other 16.

Recommended: (a), in line with "only promise what exists".
