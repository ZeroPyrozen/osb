# osb

osb (osu! Storyboarder Banquet) is the site of a community of osu! storyboarders: a showcase of their storyboards, the community list, and osb! learn, a course that teaches storyboarding.

## Language

### The course

**Course**:
The whole of osb! learn: every path, module and unit.

**Path**:
An ordered run of modules at one difficulty, finished for a trophy.
_Avoid_: course, track

**Difficulty**:
The Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced label that places a path in the course.
_Avoid_: path level, level

**Module**:
A group of units on one subject, finished for a badge.
_Avoid_: course, chapter

**Unit**:
One page of the course: a lesson, an exercise or a knowledge check.
_Avoid_: page, step, lesson (for any unit)

**Lesson**:
A unit that explains an idea with examples the learner can preview.

**Exercise**:
A unit where the learner builds a storyboard in the playground, by hand or with a script, until it passes every check.

**Check**:
One condition an exercise tests the learner's storyboard against, shown to them as a label.
_Avoid_: test

**Solution**:
An exercise's model answer, which the learner can reveal after enough real attempts.

**Knowledge check**:
A unit of multiple-choice questions, usually the last unit of a module.
_Avoid_: quiz, test

**Question**:
One multiple-choice item in a knowledge check.
_Avoid_: quiz item

**Playground**:
The in-browser editor where a learner writes a storyboard and watches it play.

**Learner**:
Someone working through osb! learn.
_Avoid_: student, user, player (a player is someone playing a beatmap in osu!)

### Outcomes

**Outcome**:
A statement of what a learner can do after a module or a path, with one Bloom level. A **module outcome** belongs to one module; a **path outcome** belongs to one path and is built by some of its modules.
_Avoid_: objective, goal, skill

**Bloom level**:
How deep an outcome goes on Bloom's revised scale: remember, understand, apply, analyse, evaluate or create.
_Avoid_: level, depth

**Topic**:
A subject a module covers, as listed on its page. Topics describe content; outcomes describe what learners can do.
_Avoid_: outcome

**Assessed**:
Said of an outcome that at least one question or exercise in its own module checks. A review may judge an outcome only partly assessed when those checks cover part of it.
_Avoid_: covered, tested

**Prerequisite**:
An earlier module that another module builds on, which a learner is advised to finish first. It is advice only and never locks a module.
_Avoid_: requirement, dependency

**Entry requirement**:
What a path expects a learner to know before starting it. Like a prerequisite, it is advice only.
_Avoid_: prerequisite (for a path)

**Review date**:
The day a unit's time-sensitive facts, such as the ranking criteria or osu!(lazer)'s behaviour, were last checked against their sources.
_Avoid_: last updated

### Rewards

**XP**:
Points a learner earns by finishing units, with bonuses for perfect first attempts and for finished modules and paths.

**Level**:
A learner's rank, reached at fixed XP thresholds, from Spectator to Banquet Wizard.
_Avoid_: rank (osu! says "ranked" for approved beatmaps)

**Badge**:
The award for finishing every unit in a module.

**Trophy**:
The award for finishing every module in a path.
_Avoid_: badge (for a path)

**Achievement**:
An award for a milestone or habit across the course, such as a first exercise or a learning streak.
_Avoid_: badge

**Perfect bonus**:
Extra XP for getting every question in a knowledge check right on the first attempt, before any feedback.
_Avoid_: perfect quiz
